I missed last year's Looptober, but I'll make a go of it this year. I'm not restricting myself to one single machine this time, anything is fine as long as it's a short piece of loopable music.

My prediction for this year is more live instrumentation and less samples, since I have a nice setup at home with a few synced loop pedals all going through a Zoom LiveTrak L6 which makes for very fast recording.

<mark class="arrow">NB</mark> These are made to be played in a loop, but if you only want a quick peek they are still playable using the `:play` command.

<mark class="arrow">NB2</mark> Loops marked with X was not finished on time, but created and added later.
